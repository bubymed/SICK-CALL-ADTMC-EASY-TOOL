# Third-party components / Componentes de terceros

- **pdf-lib 1.17.1** (`vendor/pdf-lib.min.js`) — MIT License, Copyright (c) 2019 Andrew Dillon. Full text: `vendor/pdf-lib.LICENSE.md`. Used only to draw the official DD Form 689 PDF on the device.
- **DD Form 689 (AUG 2017)** (`forms/DD689.pdf`) — U.S. Government form (public domain). Blank template copy taken from the MKRON project (https://github.com/cconner2023/test2, MIT License).
- Ideas (own implementation): PII detector and screening-to-note routing inspired by MKRON (MIT).
- **qrcode-generator 2.0.4** (`vendor/qrcode.min.js`) — MIT License, Copyright (c) 2009 Kazuhiko Arase. Full text: `vendor/qrcode.LICENSE`. Draws the QR codes (patient questionnaire result and the clinic poster) on the device.
- **jsQR 1.4.0** (`vendor/jsQR.min.js`) — Apache License 2.0, Copyright Cosmo Wolfe. Full text: `vendor/jsQR.LICENSE`. Reads the patient's QR with the camera, on the device (loaded only when you tap "Scan").
